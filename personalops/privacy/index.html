Privacy Policy

PersonalOpsAgent · Effective 23 August 2026

This policy explains how PersonalOpsAgent handles Google user data. PersonalOpsAgent is a private, self-hosted application operated by Hamza Feroze for his own authorized accounts; it is not a public consumer service.

Data accessed

Depending on the permissions granted during Google authorization, PersonalOpsAgent may access:

  • Gmail messages, message metadata, drafts, labels, and sending/reply functions;
  • Google Drive files and metadata;
  • Google Calendar calendars and events;
  • Google Docs and Google Sheets content;
  • Google Tasks task lists and tasks; and
  • Google Contacts and basic Google Account profile information.

How data is used

Google user data is used only to provide user-requested productivity functions, such as reading or sending an email, locating a file, updating a document, managing an event, or maintaining a task. PersonalOpsAgent does not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, credit decisions, or unrelated analytics.

Disclosure and sale

PersonalOpsAgent does not sell Google user data. It does not transfer Google user data to data brokers, advertising platforms, or unrelated third parties. Data may be processed by an AI service only when Hamza explicitly requests an operation requiring that processing, subject to the selected provider's terms and controls.

Storage and security

PersonalOpsAgent runs on Hamza's privately controlled Mini PC. OAuth credentials are encrypted at rest and are not published with application code. Access is restricted to Hamza's authorized environment and accounts. Operational records may be retained locally for continuity and auditing.

Retention and deletion

Locally retained data is kept only for as long as needed for the application's requested functions, history, or auditing. Hamza can remove local records and revoke Google authorization at any time through Google Account security settings. Revocation prevents future API access.

Google API Limited Use

PersonalOpsAgent's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
