PersonalOpsAgent

PersonalOpsAgent is Hamza Feroze's private, self-hosted productivity and workspace assistant. It helps Hamza work with his own email, files, calendars, documents, tasks, and contacts through Google Workspace APIs.

PersonalOpsAgent is not offered as a public service. Access is restricted to accounts explicitly authorized by Hamza.

Google Workspace access

When authorized, PersonalOpsAgent may access Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar, Docs, Sheets, Tasks, and Contacts solely to perform actions requested by Hamza. Authorization can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page.

PersonalOpsAgent is operated by Hamza Feroze. Questions about this application or its handling of Google user data can be sent to hamzaferoze115@gmail.com.
